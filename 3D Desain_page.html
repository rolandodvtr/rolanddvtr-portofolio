Shockbreaker Assembly

*Note: Original design inspired by [KTC Racing]. No copyright infringement intended

Here, I created a design for a shock absorber on a motor vehicle. Because I was curious about what a shock absorber would look like in a 3D design, I decided to make one.

For reference, I only relied on information available on the internet, such as online shops. I read the product descriptions provided by the stores and looked at the product images they provided.

Drone

*Note: Original design inspired by [DJI drone]. No copyright infringement intended

I tried to create a simple design for a drone. As before, I used the information available on the drone's official website (I used the DJI Mavic Pro as a reference in creating this design).

In making the parts, I applied a Reverse Engineering technique—a process of deconstructing and recreating a digital 3D model based on the actual dimensions and visual references of the original object.
