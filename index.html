Project-Based Learning (PBL) is a learning method that encourages students to understand material through working on real projects. In PBL, we are actively involved in the process of designing, working on, and completing a project related to real issues. This method also trains critical thinking skills, teamwork, communication, and responsibility. The college assigns this project every semester to its students, with different levels each semester.
